Antenna Analysis · 2024–Present

AAFIYA

Antenna Analysis in Frequency-domain for Impedance & Yield Assessment

A Python toolkit for reproducible antenna characterization using measurement and simulation data, including S-parameters and impedance, gain and beam patterns, beamwidth, front-to-back and cross-polarization metrics, realized vector effective length, and measurement-to-simulation validation.
